Questions

Before you ask

Are you a real estate agency?

No. WarmNest is a student support service. We help you search, translate and talk to the owner. You sign the room contract directly with the owner or a licensed agent, and the deposit and rent go to them (except package 5).

Can I pay before I arrive in Korea?

Yes. Pay the 20% from home and we start searching before you fly. For package 5 you pay the semester rent before departure.

Which universities do you cover?

Mostly Seoul and nearby: Kyung Hee, Korea University, Hanyang, Sungkyunkwan, Konkuk, Sejong and others. Tell us your university and we'll say honestly if we can help.

How big is a deposit in Korea?

A gosiwon usually needs little or no deposit. A one-room usually needs at least 2,000,000 ₩, often more. We give you real numbers before you decide.

Which languages do you speak?

Uzbek, Russian, English and Korean.

Is there a group discount?

Yes. 2 or more students who come together get 20% off packages 1–4.
